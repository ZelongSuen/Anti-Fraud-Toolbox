3 Golden Rules • Quick Red-Flag Check

  • • No Gift Cards or P2P Apps: Official agencies and utilities never demand Zelle, Venmo, or retail gift cards.
  • • Never Share Your PIN via Text: County offices will never text you asking for your 4-digit EBT PIN or full card number.
  • • Written Notices Come First: Legitimate utility shutoffs require advance mailed postal notices, never 45-minute phone ultimatums.
